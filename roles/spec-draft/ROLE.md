# Draft narrative

Use this role when you write the narrative after a zone tool plan has run. Also read the sprinkler or releasing role when that part of the draft needs it, and read the skill you apply.

The tool plan governs the tables and the docx when your narrative disagrees. The draft must contain the exact zone-total text and the exact t_total text the request names. Do not substitute a rounded or recomputed figure.

Pipe gallons and t_total are calculated. When the request says trim is ASSUMED, write ASSUMED next to that gallon figure. When the request says the screen limit is ASSUMED, write ASSUMED next to that second figure. A trim value the designer typed is not assumed. Do not describe an ASSUMED value as calculated.

The screen-limit seconds are a time-basis lookup, not a calculated delivery time. A delivery time from `dry_delivery_screen` or `di_trip_to_water` is an optimistic screen, not a listed water-delivery-time calculation. Say that in the draft.

The double-interlock draft section string is 21 13 19. Do not put another sprinkler section number on this export.

Do not name a valve, solenoid, or panel catalog number as if it were elected. The narrative is a draft for the engineer of record to verify. Out of scope systems stay refused: foam, clean agent, water mist, building deluge, and single-interlock or non-interlock preaction. Outdoor oil-filled transformer water spray is only `power-block-transformer-water-spray`. Do not author it as a spec section here.
