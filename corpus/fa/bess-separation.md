# Energy-storage room separation

NFPA 855-2023 §9.6.4 separates a room or space that contains energy storage from other areas by fire barriers and horizontal assemblies of at least 2 hours, built to the local building code. §9.6.4.1 allows 1 hour where the only systems in the room are listed to UL 9540 and marked to the UL 9540A cell-level criteria.

IFC 2024 §1207.7.4 requires 2-hour fire barriers (IBC §707) and 2-hour horizontal assemblies (IBC §711). The held IFC sentence has no 1-hour allowance.

Where both instruments apply, design practice uses 2 hours. The 1-hour NFPA 855 allowance is available only when the listing mark is in hand and the fire code does not govern it away. Verify with the engineer of record before using 1 hour.

Boundary dampers and door releases sit on that rated line. Gas exhaust for the room is not shut down by a general HVAC shutdown that serves the rest of the building. Detection inside the room is `ess-room-detection.md`.
