# Cybersecurity on the fire alarm (NFPA 72 Chapter 11)

Chapter 11 applies when a law, a regulation, or another part of NFPA 72 requires it. NFPA 72-2022 and 2025 §24.1.3 also pull Chapter 11 onto an emergency communications system. The 2025 annex states that cybersecurity is required only when another section, an authority, or a regulation mandates it. Do not add a security level because a hall has a network.

## Levels and pathways

NFPA 72-2025 §11.1.2 exempts a system that meets both listed conditions from a minimum cybersecurity level. Read those conditions in the enforced edition before claiming the exemption.

§11.3 sets security levels SL1 through SL3 by interface. SL3 covers an IP interface, wired or wireless, that connects to a publicly accessible network (§11.3(3)).

§11.4 protects the pathways with metal raceways or metal armored cable. The 2025 annex also recommends that protection for Security Level 1. §11.7 sends any data connection to an external network through a gateway or firewall that passes only trusted traffic.

A link from the fire alarm control unit to a building-management or operations network is an external connection under §11.7. Which security level applies is an owner and engineer-of-record call. Record the level in the Chapter 7 documentation (§11.12).

## Upkeep

Install the manufacturer's required security updates at least annually (§11.8.5). The tester checks certificate validity annually (§11.11.2).

The 2022 Chapter 11 body was not compared line by line with 2025. Where the job is on 2022, confirm the section before copying a 2025 level.
