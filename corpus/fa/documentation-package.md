# Fire alarm documentation package

When the authority requires documentation, NFPA 72 §7.2.1 is the minimum list. The 2019, 2022, and 2025 editions each carry 17 items. Cite §7.2.1 the same way on all three. The 2019 list also points at the §7.8.2 forms. The 2022 and 2025 lists point at §7.5.6 and §7.6.6 without that extra forms cite.

The package the designer owns includes the narrative, the riser, the floor plans, the sequence of operation, the battery calculation, the notification-circuit voltage drop, the audibility targets, the candela layout, the pathway diagrams, and the record of completion. Air-sampling transport time comes from the listed pipe-network program, not from a hand estimate.

## Sequence and shop drawings

The sequence of operation is a narrative or an input/output matrix. It ships with the shop drawings (NFPA 72 §7.4.9) and again with the record drawings so the matrix matches the program that was loaded (§7.5.5.2). Those two section numbers and that duty are the same in 2019, 2022, and 2025.

Shop-drawing identification is §7.4.4, items (1) through (5): premises, installer, location, the NFPA 170 legend, and issue dates. Item (4) is the legend. It is not the sequence. The sequence stays at §7.4.9. The §7.4.4 list is the same in 2019, 2022, and 2025.

§7.4.3 points record drawings at §7.5.2 in 2019 and 2022, and at §7.5.5 in 2025. Floor-plan content is §7.4.5. The 2019 list is numbered (1) through (15). The 2022 and 2025 lists add pathway class and survivability.

## Record of completion

The contractor gives the record of completion to the authority and the owner, and updates it when the system changes (§7.5.6). On 2019 and 2022, a new record after revision is §7.5.6.6.3. On 2025 that duty is §7.5.6.6.4. An electronic record needs authority approval and a backup (§7.5.6.7).

Designer contact information is §7.2.2. Symbols follow NFPA 170 (§7.2.3). Both are the same duty in 2019, 2022, and 2025.

Layout documentation for notification performance sits in §7.3.4. The title is Notification in 2019 and 2022. The audible test rows in Chapter 14 that say "required performance, as documented per 7.3.4" mean that section. There is no separate section titled required performance.

On an FM job, add the early-warning layout, the ventilation-interlock test, the power-isolation plan, and the two double-interlock flags in `di-releasing.md`. Those are insurer and owner records. They are not extra §7.2.1 items.

Emergency-communications owner's manual content in NFPA 72-2025 §7.5.4, and the three verification ensures in §7.5.8.3, apply when that chapter is in the basis. Confirm them on a 2019 or 2022 job before citing the 2025 numbers.
