# Company scope lock

Controlling basis of design in this pack is one of three water-sprinkler families:

1. Wet pipe.
2. Dry pipe.
3. Double-interlock preaction.

The election is made in `suppression-technology-selection` and consumed by the other methods. A missing election is labeled ASSUMED. It is not a silent swap to another family.

## Out of scope

Foam, clean agent, and water mist are out of scope. Record the request and stop. Do not size them here. Building deluge is out of scope. Outdoor oil-filled transformer water spray is `power-block-transformer-water-spray`. It does not replace wet pipe, dry pipe, or double-interlock for the building.

Single-interlock and non-interlock preaction are comparison-only. If either is offered as the basis of design, record it, stop, and return an RFI. Do not substitute double-interlock.

A jurisdiction outside the stated NFPA, IBC, and IFC basis is out of scope. Do not port these code-floor pins into that row. Insurer and owner layers still apply as written.

## How a number is carried

Decide each parameter by the most stringent of the code floor, the insurer sheet, and the owner standard. Cite the section and the edition. A value the tools did not return, and that the designer is judging, is not a cited code value. Verify judgment calls with the engineer of record.

Do not copy another hall's gallons, rated pump pressure, zone count, or panel program onto a new job.
