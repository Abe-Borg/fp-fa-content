# Smoke stratification and high ceilings

Run a stratification screen when the ceiling is above about 30 ft, or when a warm layer can form under the deck. Bands that should trigger the screen are 30 ft to 50 ft, then above 50 ft, then above 100 ft for automated storage.

NFPA 72 requires spacing to account for the plume, the ceiling jet, and the airflow that is already in the room. The numerical screen is Annex B. Re-pin the worked example to the enforced edition before you cite it. In the example held with this note, a 39 °C floor-to-ceiling difference and a 106 kW fire leave smoke at 9.5 m (about 31 ft). Treat 39 °C as a bound in that example, not as a design default. With no temperature rise, fires of at least 106 kW reach ceiling detectors at heights up to 9.5 m in the same figure.

A gradient near 0.55 °C per meter stranded a 10 kW fire in one guide model. The captions are not consistent with that slope, so use 0.55 only as an order of magnitude. At that slope, a 40 ft hall needs only about 7 °C from floor to ceiling to strand that small fire. A full-scale test at a 43 m ceiling with about 12 °C of difference (about 0.28 °C per meter) still showed smoke at the ceiling. Screen on the gradient, not on a single temperature difference.

FM DS 5-48 §2.2.3.2.5 and Figure 6 (January 2011 / interim April 2025) show lower-elevation smoke detectors where a heat barrier can keep smoke off a high ceiling.

Tall-storage sampling geometry used in one European practice note: points no more than 6 m apart horizontally; racks over 8 m get a sample in the top quarter of the rack and within 10 m of the ceiling; further levels at most 8 m apart vertically, each offset from the level below; aisles narrower than 3 m do not need both sides; an intermediate plane needs a hole in every aisle. Confirm that text before it leaves the note as a requirement. It is not an NFPA 72 spacing table.

A federal design criteria document reduces smoke-detection area by 50 percent where destratification fans are used. That reduction is not the unamended I-Code pin (`ufc-outside-pin.md`). Re-run the screen with the fans off. The alarm sequence can remove the mixing that was holding smoke at the ceiling.

Screen with the small fire the early-warning tier is meant to catch. A 500 kW fire reached the ceiling in both thermal cases of one study at 90 seconds, which does not prove the small fire will.

NFPA 72 Annex B.4.5 states there is no quantitative hand method for dilution or for airflow effects on location. A hall past the air-change table is a performance layout (`../fa/asd-performance.md`). Do not invent a spacing formula from a computational smoke model and call it Chapter 17.
