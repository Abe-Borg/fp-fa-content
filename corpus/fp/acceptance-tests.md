# Acceptance tests for water-based systems

NFPA 25 is the post-turnover regime. It is not the acceptance basis. IFC §901.5 requires acceptance tests. IFC §901.6.2 indexes integrated testing. Confirm the adopted edition. The fire-alarm integrated-test team is `../fa/acceptance-testing.md`.

Aboveground sprinkler pipe: hydrostatic test at least 200 psi for 2 hours, or 50 psi above static when static exceeds 150 psi, for 2 hours. Confirm the paragraph in the adopted NFPA 13. New underground pipe on an impairment closeout uses the same 200 psi for 2 hours, or working pressure plus 50 psi, then a flush (FM DS 10-7 §2.2.4.1.B).

Dry or preaction pipe: 40 psi for at least 24 hours, and correct a loss over 1.5 psi, counted for the whole system (FM DS 2-0 §2.7.3.2.8; NFPA 13-2022 and 2025 §29.2.2.1). See `nitrogen-sampling.md`.

A new hall proves the 30 second delivery with a witnessed timing test. A volume calculation is corroboration only (FM DS 5-32 §2.4.3.2.1.2(D)). The form records delivery without the quick-opening device and ends the clock at design pressure at the most remote sprinkler (FM DS 2-0 Appendix C, form used for that test). First water at the inspector's test is not that endpoint (`water-delivery-screen.md`).

One carrier's in-service trip passes or fails at 60 seconds from full opening of the inspector's test valve. That 60 seconds is not the FM 30 second remote-sprinkler clock. Grade each clock to the instrument that owns it.

Heated-wire early-warning acceptance is `vewfd-acceptance.md`. Air-sampling transport on an FM hall remains 60 seconds.

NFPA 20 acceptance: at least 150 percent of rated capacity at not less than 65 percent of rated head, and churn head at most 140 percent of rated head (NFPA 20-2025 §6.2; the same 1.40 and 65 percent limits appear in the hydraulic note). A federal pump specification that hydrostatically tests the pump at 225 psi for 2 hours and requires 10 automatic starts plus 10 manual starts is that specification. It is not the unamended I-Code pin.

Hold installation until drawings, calculations, and specifications are accepted, and review them again after every revision (FM DS 2-0 §2.6.1.2).

Shop drawings and calculations are sealed by the engineer of record. A layout technician works under that seal. Do not treat a NICET level as a substitute for the seal. Working plans are NFPA 13-2022 and 2025 §28.1. Older checklists that cite Chapter 23 must be remapped. Local rules change hydrant spacing, flow-test age, safety factor, and connection location. Do not copy another city's numbers onto the plans.

Fast-response sprinklers on one carrier sheet are sampled at 20 years, then every 10 years. The sample is 2 per floor, 1 percent, or 4 heads, whichever is greater. Stock enough spares to replace the hydraulic design area. A 3,500 ft² hall is on the order of 27 to 35 heads against an NFPA 13 spare-cabinet tier of 24. Confirm the cabinet tier in the adopted NFPA 13. The carrier's 3,000 ft² illustration is not the hall area.
