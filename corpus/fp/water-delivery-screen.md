# Dry and double-interlock water-delivery screen

Accelerator trip time is not water-delivery time. A listed quick-opening device can shorten the valve-open part of the clock. It does not prove water at the remote sprinkler. Do not claim a 30 second or 60 second delivery unless the caller names the listed calculation program, or a witnessed test measures water at the remote point. The optimistic screen in `calcs/fp` (`t = t_detect + volume / fill flow`) is a screen. It is not a listed water-delivery calculation. A zone that fails that screen is unproved. A zone that passes is not proved.

## Clocks (do not blend them)

- NFPA 13 general inspection-test route is on the order of 60 seconds at one point. That sentence is not the data-hall insurer gate.
- Ordinary-hazard calculated analogue held in the pack: 50 seconds, two most remote sprinklers (NFPA 13-2025 Table 8.2.4.3.2; 2019 Table 8.2.3.6.1). The 2007 table wording is `[V]`.
- Where FM DS 5-32 January 2026 §2.4.3.2.1.2(D) applies, the data-center target is 30 seconds to the single most remote sprinkler. That is the insurer criterion, not the NFPA code floor. The sheet's lack of an interlock-type qualifier is comparison-only. It does not authorize a single-interlock basis of design.
- FM DS 2-0 (October 2021 / interim January 2024) §2.2.1.11.1 is a generic ladder that applies only when the occupancy sheet does not set a maximum. DS 5-32 does set 30 seconds, so 30 seconds governs wherever that sheet governs.
- A carrier white paper can add a double-interlock area increase and a nitrogen-fill requirement without stating a 30 second clock. Do not borrow 30 seconds from FM onto that carrier.

## Volume caps

Double-interlock does not inherit the dry-pipe 750 gallon quick-opening exemption.

- Double-interlock, NFPA 13 §8.3.2.3.1.1 (2019, 2022, and 2025): at most 500 gallons, or 60 seconds, or a listed calculation. No 750 gallon path in that section.
- Dry pipe quick-opening path: NFPA 13-2025 §8.2.4.2.2; NFPA 13-2022 §8.2.3.4 (750 gallons). That path is dry pipe only.
- An inspection checkbox that mentions dry systems over 750 gallons is an inspection trigger. It is not an FM design waiver.
- Where FM DS 5-32 applies, keep the data-center zone at or under 500 gallons and at or under 30 seconds. A zone over 500 gallons does not take the dry-pipe 750 gallon exemption.

Gallons come from the pipe takeoff (`fp/pipe-volume.md`). Do not copy another layout's gallon target.
