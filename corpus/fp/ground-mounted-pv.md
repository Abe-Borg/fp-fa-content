# Ground-mounted and carport photovoltaic arrays

Roof-mounted modules are `roof-mounted-pv.md`. This note is a ground array, a tracker farm, or a carport, including arrays about 7 ft above grade that the roof sheet hands off. The sheet is FM DS 7-106, interim April 2026. Floating arrays are outside both sheets. The array is not a data-hall density.

Fire-department access is a road at least 20 ft wide to the site and around the perimeter. A row or string longer than 600 ft gets additional roads at hose-reach intervals (§2.1.1.10). Separate the array from transformers, energy storage, and other combustibles on those occupancy sheets (`transformer-siting.md`, `ess-room-sprinklers.md`, `campus-exterior-exposure.md`). Bifacial modules are a noncombustible exposure. Monofacial modules are a combustible exposure (§2.1.1.5).

Wind: importance factor 1.15, connection safety factor at least 2.0, Exposure C typical, and no ballasted ground mount (§2.1.2). Trackers get at least two anemometers on each side and stow at 25 percent below the instability speed (§2.1.2.4). The annual anemometer test is at or below 35 mph (§2.4.4). A carport uses the ASCE figures the sheet names. A vehicle taller than half the clear height leaves the clear-flow coefficients (§2.1.2.5.1).

Hail ratings in Table 2.1.3.2: moderate 1.5 in and 8 ft-lb, severe 1.75 in and 14 ft-lb, very severe 2.5 in and 53 ft-lb. The roof sheet has no very-severe path. Variable-tilt arrays stow from a weather service, starting at least 30 minutes before arrival plus the stow time, and drill at least quarterly (§2.5.2).

Seismic: Risk Category III and importance 1.25 as a minimum, component importance 1.5, and IEEE 693 high or moderate by the return period in §2.1.5. Shake performance is 2 times or 4 times the required response spectrum, damping at most 2 percent, ground-founded only.

Flood: equipment and access roads 1 ft to 2 ft above the 500-year flood where practical (§2.1.6). Vegetation stays at or below 6 in, and cuttings are removed (§2.1.7.4.1). Modules are ASTM E108 Class A or Class B for flame spread and burning brand. Modules built with plastic insulation need an exterior-fire approval. Wildland exposure is `campus-exterior-exposure.md`.

Electrical protection is the adopted NEC Article 690, including ground-fault protection, overcurrent protection, and surge protection on both sides of the inverter. Insulation resistance is checked about every 3 years (Table 2.4.8.2.1). A fuse-only ground-fault scheme can miss the first fault. Transformers beside the array are `transformer-siting.md`.

The three-layer method is `most-stringent-basis-matrix`. This sheet does not set a sprinkler density.
