# Grouped cable and cable trays

Hall trays that only obstruct a data-hall ceiling stay on `hall-geometry.md` (at least 12 psi, or a head within 4 ft). This note is a cable vault, a tray room, a tunnel, or an electrical room whose significant combustible is grouped cable. The sheet is FM DS 5-31, January 2020, interim October 2025.

A cable-only area takes ceiling sprinklers at 0.2 gpm/ft² over 3,000 ft², 165 °F heads, and 130 ft² spacing, installed to DS 2-0, with the trays inside the spray pattern (§2.3.2.2.B). Other combustibles in the same area send the density to that occupancy sheet. The cable-only package does not apply alone (§2.3.2.2.C). Oil breakers, transformers, storage, and the data hall are those other cases.

Obstructed high trays get a sprinkler in every aisle or walkway (§2.3.2.2.1). A tunnel or an outdoor tray run is 0.2 gpm/ft² over the most remote 100 linear feet, up to 3,000 ft² (§2.3.2.2.2). Outdoor trays use line-type heat detection in the tray (§2.3.3). Indoor cable spaces still get smoke detection. Smoke does not omit the sprinklers.

Gaseous protection and water mist on this sheet are out of scope. The water path above is the company basis. Passive alternatives the sheet allows are FM Approved GP-1 cable or an FM 3971 wrap or coating (§2.3.2.1). Those are not a sprinkler design.

Critical cable that can see an occupancy fire is routed on an independent path, buried, concrete-encased, or put in an enclosure of at least 1 hour that extends 20 ft past the fire area. An FM 3973 exposure wrap uses the same 20 ft extension (§2.3.1). That wrap is not the FM 3971 passive wrap.

Redundant feeds stay on independent routes (§2.1.1.5). Power trays stay separate from control and signal trays. Noncombustible trays. Highest voltage on top of a stack. Firestops at a barrier match the barrier rating (§2.1.1). Tray-to-tray spacing is Table 2.1.2.4. A medium-voltage room that also has grouped cable adds this density on top of the room package (`mv-switchgear-room.md`).

The method for the room that contains the trays is `mv-switchgear-room-package` when the object is switchgear, and `power-block-transformer-water-spray` when the exposure is an outdoor transformer.
