# Ignitable-liquid areas: water sprinklers, drainage, containment

Foam, clean agent, and building water spray are out of scope. This note is plain-water ceiling sprinklers, drainage, and containment. If the request is an agent system, record it and stop. Outdoor transformer apparatus water spray is a different system (`../scope-lock.md`). Do not size it from this note.

Classify by closed-cup flash point before picking a density. Diesel is FM Group A, flash point under 200 °F, and is not very-high flash point (FM DS 7-32A, April 2026, §1.1 and §2.1.2.1). Very-high flash point, unheated, is a closed-cup flash point of at least 414 °F (212 °C). Above 5,000 ft, correct the flash point before grouping.

Flash point under 200 °F with drainage: 0.30 gpm/ft² over 4,000 ft² with high-temperature heads, or 6,000 ft² with ordinary heads, 500 gpm hose, 60 minutes, K at least 8.0, roof at most 40 ft (FM DS 7-32 Table 2.4.3, April 2020 / interim April 2026). Flash point of at least 200 °F with drainage uses that same control package. Without drainage the extinguishment density runs from 0.30 to 0.80 by roof height, K at least 11.2.

Very-high flash point with shutoffs and no spray may use the surrounding occupancy, or at least 0.2 gpm/ft² over 2,500 ft². Specific gravity above 1 stays on the 0.30 gpm/ft² extinguishment row. An explosion-hazard room on that table is 0.30 gpm/ft² over 6,000 ft² to 8,000 ft² with 1,000 gpm hose for 120 minutes.

Do not use the 0.30 / 4,000 package on drums. Metal 55 gallon racks are 50 sprinklers at 7 psi, K at least 11.2, high-temperature, ceiling at most 30 ft (FM DS 7-29 Table 2.4.3.1, October 2020 / interim July 2024). FM DS 7-29 §3.5.2 does not recommend dry systems for ignitable-liquid storage without a full-scale test, and it states no fixed dry-area penalty. A double-interlock election there needs that test, or it is not supported by the sheet.

A continuous large release leaves Table 2.4.3. Indoor protection is then 0.30 gpm/ft² over 10,000 ft², water for at least 2 hours, and hose of at least 500 gpm (FM DS 7-14, April 2026).

One carrier sheet (PRC.8.1.0, 2020) sizes water-only tank-building sprinklers at 0.60 gpm/ft² over any 5,000 ft², including the most remote, with 286 °F heads. Ceiling heads over ignitable liquid on that sheet are high-temperature. Containment is the greater of the largest tank plus the designed fire-protection discharge, or 110 percent of the largest tank. FM DS 7-83 dropped hose from drain capacity. Name the carrier before you size the drain. Do not take design credit for an active system when the control exists only because that system might fail (PRC.8.1.0).

Adequate drainage allows a 3 in. curb. Without it, curb height is 1.6 times Q divided by A, plus 2 in., with Q in gallons and A in square feet (FM DS 7-83, January 2012 / interim January 2015, §2.2.1.9 and §2.2.1.11). Default trench inflow is 30 gpm per foot from one side or 60 gpm per foot from both sides, and free trench area is at least three times the outlet area (§2.2.4.1). An individual trench on the carrier sheet is 1.5 times density times area. Floor slope toward the trenches is at least 1 percent.

Hot work near ignitable liquid is `impairment-and-hot-work.md`. Gas detection setpoints for a process area are `gas-detection.md`.
