# Meet-me rooms and main distribution frames

A private information-technology room stays on NFPA 75 and FM DS 5-32. A room that provides public telecommunications or carrier continuity is the NFPA 76 question. The detection tier for a meet-me room is already one line in `../fa/space-detection-matrix.md`. This note is the regime test and the suppression overlay.

NFPA 76-2024 sizes the signal-processing area as the energized equipment, the access aisles, and a 2 ft perimeter (§6.1.3). Above 2,500 ft² of that area, the signal-processing area and the main distribution frame take very-early-warning fire detection (§6.8.6.1 and §6.11.6.1). At or below 2,500 ft² the tier is early warning. The 2,500 ft² test aggregates signal-processing area on all floors of the facility. Several small rooms can push every signal-processing room and frame room in the facility up to very early warning.

When any of the first six area types in the NFPA 76 list share one enclosed room, the most restrictive detection applies to the whole room. Standby engines are outside that merge. A combined frame-and-power room therefore inherits very-early-warning detection across the power side as well.

Very-early-warning spacing that matches the data-hall architecture is `../fa/asd-performance.md`. Raised-floor and return-air ports follow the NFPA 76 rows for common airflow. Alert stays supervisory (`../fa/space-detection-matrix.md`).

NFPA 76 can allow omission or a limited suppression path for some equipment levels, with a 1-hour separation still required. On an FM campus the water basis governs unless the owner elects the code-minimum path and the insurer and the authority accept it in writing. The hall numbers are `density-area.md`. Gaseous agent is out of scope.

FM DS 5-14, April 2012, interim April 2025, covers telephone central offices and DC-powered switching, and it sends voice-over-internet-protocol and AC information technology to DS 5-32 (§1.0). Do not import a central-office density into a hall, and do not import the hall density into a true central office, until the power type is named.

A multitenant building that the telecommunications provider does not control takes the NFPA 76 multitenant separation, 2 hours from the rest of the building (§6.1.4). A federal service-footprint test that asks whether the room serves more than one facility is `ufc-electronic-equipment.md`. That test is Defense criteria.

The detection method is `fa-asd-vewfd-data-hall-sampling-layout`. The suppression method, once the room stays on the hall sheet, is `density-area-dry-increase`.
