# Roof-mounted photovoltaic arrays

Ground arrays and carports, including modules about 7 ft above grade, are `ground-mounted-pv.md`. This note is modules on a building roof. The sheet is FM DS 1-15, April 2026 full revision. It is not a data-hall density.

An FM Approved Class 1A roof loses that rating once modules sit above it, unless the assembly is approved as a roof-photovoltaic system (§2.0 and §3.2). Component tests of the roof and of the module do not preserve the rating. Thin-film adhered modules take the lower of the roof class and the module class. Adhered flexible modules are not currently FM Approved (§3.3). An existing roof older than 10 years is replaced or recovered before a 25- to 30-year array (§2.1).

New work prefers an approved roof-photovoltaic assembly. Where the existing cover stays, the sheet's paths are an approved same-component array, an approved retrofit coating, a Class 1A recover, glass-backed modules on an approved metal roof, or a raise of at least 3 ft (§2.1.1.2). A raise of more than 2 ft invalidates catalog wind coefficients (§3.4.14). The 3 ft fire raise therefore needs its own wind study.

The sheet also offers under-panel water spray at 0.07 gpm/ft² (§2.1.1.3). That is building water spray. It is out of scope here. The outdoor-transformer carve-out in `../scope-lock.md` does not cover a roof array. Do not import 0.07 into a hall calculation.

Array limits: 150 ft by 150 ft, aisles and expansion joints at least 4 ft, and skylights at least 6 ft (§2.1.1.4). Glass-backed modules and noncombustible frames. No rubber or plastic pedestal pads. An aisle break adds about 50 percent to ballast or securement on the adjacent panels (§3.4.2).

Wind safety factor is 2.0 and the importance factor is 1.15 (§2.1.2). Ballast is only on adhered roofs at or below 1/2 in per foot, with friction coefficient at most 0.4, and not on aggregate (§2.1.2.3). Hail Table 2.1.3.1-1 has a moderate and a severe class. Very severe hail is not available on this sheet. The ground-array sheet is the one with a very-severe rating.

Seismic restraint is positive fastening. Friction-only ballast is not the restraint (§2.1.4). Flex cable exceeds 2 in. Do not add ballast beyond the wind demand and call it seismic resistance.

Commission above 0.5 MW (§2.3.1). Thermography at startup and again at 14 days, then every 3 years. Fasteners every 3 years. Electrical visual every 6 months. Roof penetration seals annually. Interior cable is `cable-trays.md`. Yard exposure of the building face is `campus-exterior-exposure.md`.

The three-layer method is `most-stringent-basis-matrix`.
