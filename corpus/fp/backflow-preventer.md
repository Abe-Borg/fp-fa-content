# Backflow preventers on the fire service

A backflow preventer spends pressure, caps the system at its own rating, and creates a permanent full-flow test. Decide all three at schematic design. The device tier is set by the water purveyor and the plumbing code, not by NFPA. Antifreeze, a non-potable tank, or a private main of unknown quality can force a reduced-pressure zone assembly. Ask the purveyor in writing. The answer changes the pressure budget by about 10 psi and whether the room needs a drain.

Not every fire main needs a preventer. One carrier sheet disagrees with adding one only because the pipe is black steel, unless the authority requires it. Follow the purveyor when they require it. Do not invent the device for the pipe material.

## Three different psi figures

| Figure | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| 5 psi | FM DS 3-0 §3.3. A screen for whether the fitting loss is large enough to count. Not an allowance |
| 12 psi reduced-pressure, 8 psi double check | A federal criterion (UFC 3-600-01 §9-6). A planning floor until the certified curve is in the calculation. Not the unamended I-Code pin |
| The manufacturer's curve at the fire flow | The loss to submit. One family spans about 4 to 26 psi by size and flow. A carrier typical is 4 to 6 psi for a double-check valve assembly and at least 10 psi for a reduced-pressure zone at rated flow |

Never carry 5 psi for a reduced-pressure zone. Plan on the federal floor when that criterion applies, and replace it with the certified loss for the size and the configuration before the calculation is submitted. A 10 to 15 psi allowance from a secondary discussion is the same warning. It is not a fourth design value. Do not quote a loss from this note.

## Where it goes, and at what flow

Prefer the discharge side of a booster. Count the loss there. NFPA 20 is against devices that restrict the suction. If the purveyor forces a suction-side location, redo the suction analysis at the device's loss at 150 percent of rated pump flow, not at the duty point. Keep it, and any butterfly, clear of the suction flange (`control-valves.md`, `fire-pump-installation.md`).

Size the assembly on the fire flow. Manufacturers publish a service flow near 7.5 ft/s, a continuous rated flow, and a listing flow at 150 percent of that rated flow which is not a continuous-duty rating. The fire-pump acceptance point is the 150 percent case. Carry the friction at that flow, which is the top of the curve. A chart drawn out to 25 ft/s is not permission to design at 25 ft/s. One cited manual caps service velocity at 10 ft/s. Confirm the current manual (`[V]`).

The test header is downstream of the preventer, or the annual test does not flow through the device. FM DS 2-81 Table 5 is a full-flow test above the greatest sprinkler demand, every year, with an internal inspection every 5 years. A carrier sheet adds a recorded pressure drop at a flow that represents the fire demand, and the same 5 year internal look. Design the drainage now. An impairment every year to find a place for the water is a failed layout (`pump-power-testing.md`).

A reduced-pressure zone dumps through the relief valve. Provide the air gap. One carrier dimension is twice the supply diameter and at least 1 in. Size the floor drain or the sump for the relief discharge, not for a trickle. Do not put the assembly in a pit or below grade. A submerged relief opening is a new cross connection. The listing temperature on one family starts at 33 °F, which is above freezing. A heated enclosure or an indoor room is required where the site freezes (`cold-climate-freeze.md`).

Check the assembly's maximum working pressure against churn, not against the street. A 175 psi assembly becomes the pressure ceiling of a system that was otherwise headed for 250 or 300 psi components. Read the approval scope. A family mark on the shutoff valves is not an FM Approval of the assembly, and one configuration can be excluded. UL and FM do not, on the carrier's reading, test the backflow performance itself. The purveyor may still require a foundation or ASSE listing.

Supervise both shutoff valves. The two-turn switch rule is `waterflow-supervision.md`. A flood sensor specified without its connection kit is a passive part. A cloud alert is not a fire-alarm point. Treat it as a building-management signal, and still size the drain.

If the jockey discharges downstream of the fire-pump preventer, it may need its own device. A carrier Class 2 suction target of 200 percent of rated capacity at 20 psi is that carrier. It is stricter than a 10 psi caution in an older cross-connection manual. On an FM job the positive-suction rules in `fire-pump-installation.md` still govern.
