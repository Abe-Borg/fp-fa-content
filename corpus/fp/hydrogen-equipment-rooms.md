# Hydrogen storage and equipment rooms

Open this note for bulk hydrogen, an electrolyzer, an indoor hydrogen equipment room, or an owner-elected hydrogen fuel-cell supply. Battery-room gassing is `battery-room-hydrogen.md`. Lithium-ion off-gas is `ess-explosion-control.md`. The sheet is FM DS 7-91, January 2021, interim October 2025. A campus that has only battery hydrogen does not use this sheet.

Bulk storage stays outdoors (§2.1.2.1). Indoor gaseous cylinders are at most 2,000 scf. Indoor liquid dewars are at most 50 gal (§2.1.2.2 and §2.1.2.3). Keep them near the point of use, or use gas cabinets. Stay at least 20 ft from the plane under overhead power lines (§2.1.2.5). Separation to buildings, wall openings, and air intakes is Tables 2.1.2.6-1 and 2.1.2.6-2. Intakes are the long legs: mid-band gaseous storage wants 50 ft to an intake, and the top band wants 100 ft. A liquefied store at the top band is 120 ft from a building. Coordinate economizer and generator-combustion intakes before the yard is frozen. Transformers near the yard take the longer of DS 5-4 and that table (`transformer-siting.md`).

An indoor equipment room is a special-hazard room. It has an exterior wall and damage-limiting construction (`damage-limiting-construction.md`), 2-hour floors, walls, and ceilings, and self-closing doors (§2.1.4.1.1). Continuous ventilation is at least 1 cfm/ft² (§2.1.4.1.2). That number also appears on a battery-room sheet for a different gas. Name the sheet that matches the chemistry. Without gas cabinets, detection at 25 percent of the lower explosive limit starts 2.5 cfm/ft² of emergency exhaust and a hydrogen shutoff (§2.1.4.1.3 and §2.1.4.1.5). An energy-storage room that trips at 10 percent stays at 10 percent (`../fa/ess-room-detection.md`). Detector power and calibration are `gas-detection.md`.

Ceiling sprinklers are at least hazard category 2 through DS 3-26 (§2.4.2.1). This sheet prints no density. Electrical classification is Class I, Division or Zone 2, Group B within 15 ft of gaseous hydrogen and 25 ft of liquefied hydrogen, and Division or Zone 1 within 3 ft of make-or-break connections (§2.4.1).

Piping into the building is ASME B31.12, with an excess-flow valve at the bulk shutoff and a restrictor before entry (§2.2.3). An electrolyzer enclosure uses lightweight construction or panels that fail at or below 20 psf (§2.1.2.8.4).

A hydrogen flame can be invisible. Shut off the release before extinguishment is attempted (§2.5.2.1). Fuel-cell machine protection is not in this sheet. Gaseous agent and water mist are out of scope.

The gas-detection method is `fa-bess-gas-detection-layout-and-setpoints` only for an energy-storage room. This room uses the 25 percent trip above.
