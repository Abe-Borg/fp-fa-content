# Lithium-ion modules stored or staged on campus

Installed racks in the hall are `li-ion-bbu.md`. A dedicated energy-storage room is `ess-room-sprinklers.md`. Modules in boxes, on pallets, or in a receiving and swap room are FM DS 7-112. The method that reads the storage design is `storage-staging-sprinkler-design`. This note is the state-of-charge gate that skill consumes.

Incidental storage is at most 200 ft², at most 6 ft high, aisles at least 10 ft, and state of charge at most 60 percent, in metal or cardboard containers (§2.4.3.1). Unexpanded plastic containers are protected as low-piled unexpanded uncartoned plastic, Table 2.4.3.2. Incidental hose is 500 gpm for 1 hour (§2.4.3).

Finished product at or below 60 percent state of charge follows DS 8-9 for the commodity, excluding the battery hazard (§2.4.4.1). Above 60 percent, add ceiling and in-rack protection (§2.4.4.2).

New or refurbished storage that the tables accept is at most 60 percent state of charge, ceiling at most 40 ft, storage at most 15 ft, racks at most three tiers, and quick-response only (Tables 2.4.5.1-1 and 2.4.5.1-2). Separate other combustibles by at least 10 ft (§2.4.5.3). Aisles are at least 10 ft. Abutted pile width is at most 15 ft (§2.4.5.4). A ceiling-only option has no storage above the batteries (§2.4.5.2).

In-rack flow is 60 gpm from the six most remote sprinklers with one barrier, or the eight most remote with two or more barriers (§2.4.2.2.5). Minimum pressure is 10 psi at K at least 11.2, and 7 psi below that K. Vertical spacing is 6 ft in formation and aging, and 12 ft in storage. Barriers are plywood at least 3/8 in or sheet metal at least 22 gauge. Ceiling demand is excluded from the in-rack calculation (§2.4.2.2.6).

Damaged or returned modules indoors go in a cutoff room, one pallet high, at 0.3 gpm/ft² over the room, with a door directly outside (§2.4.6.3). Outdoors they stay within 900 ft² and two pallets, 10 ft between piles, 10 ft from a noncombustible wall and 20 ft from a combustible wall (§2.4.6.2). Hose for that stock is 500 gpm for 2 hours.

Automated storage is wet only. At or below 30 percent state of charge is the first gate. From 30 to 60 percent adds metal-lined containers of at least 18 gauge, horizontal loading, and both ceiling and in-rack sprinklers. Above 60 percent is prohibited (§2.4.7). Dry pipe and double-interlock are a poor fit for that wet-only row. If the company election is dry or double-interlock, verify with the engineer of record before using this occupancy. Do not silently swap the hall family.

Obstructions wider than 3 ft or larger than 10 ft² need sprinklers underneath (§2.4.1.2.1). Hood ventilation, where the process has one, is 1 cfm/ft² normally and 1.5 times that at 25 percent of the lower explosive limit, nonrecirculating (§2.3.2.1). Manufacturing, formation, and warehouse storage are separated by 1-hour noncombustible walls (§2.2.2).

Water mist and gaseous agent are out of scope.
