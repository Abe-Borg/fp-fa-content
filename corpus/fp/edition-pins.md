# Edition pins (unamended 2024 I-Codes)

These are reference pins from `most-stringent-basis-matrix`. They are not the enforced text until the project's adopted instrument is verified. Do not cite a latest edition as the code minimum unless the authority having jurisdiction adopted it.

| Standard | 2024-cycle enforced reference | Latest / delta reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NFPA 13 | 2022 | 2025 |
| NFPA 72 | 2022 | 2025 |
| NFPA 20 | 2022 | 2025 |
| NFPA 25 | 2023 | 2026 |
| NFPA 70 (NEC) | 2023, resolve on its own | 2026 |
| NFPA 75 | no direct Chapter 80 / 35 pin | 2024 |
| NFPA 855 | 2023 best-supported reference | 2026 |

Example of a code-floor chain: IFC 2024 §903.3.1.1 points at NFPA 13-2022 until a local amendment says otherwise. FM DS 5-32 January 2026 is an insurer sheet, not that code floor.

NFPA 13-2025 is the delta baseline. Two nearby sections are different rules and must be cited with the edition:

- Dry-pipe and double-interlock area increase of 30 percent: NFPA 13-2025 §19.2.3.2.6, NFPA 13-2022 §19.2.3.2.5, NFPA 13-2019 §19.3.3.2.5.
- Ceilings over 30 ft: NFPA 13-2025 §19.2.3.2.5. In the 2022 edition that same section number is the dry / double-interlock increase, not the ceiling rule.

When a 2025 section is not pinned, report a directional delta and mark the row `[V]`. Do not fabricate a percentage.
