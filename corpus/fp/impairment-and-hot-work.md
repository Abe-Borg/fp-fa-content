# Impairment and hot work

## Impairment

FM DS 10-7 (April 2019 / interim October 2025) §2.2.2.3 limits an impairment to the smallest area the work allows. The sheet states no fixed advance-notice hour and no one-at-a-time rule. A carrier impairment center that wants 48 hours' notice and only one impairment at a time is that carrier's rule. Do not attribute it to FM.

Close sprinkler work with a 2 in. drain test downstream of each valve that was closed (§2.2.4.1.A). New underground pipe is hydrostatically tested and flushed (§2.2.4.1.B; numbers in `acceptance-tests.md`).

A normally open valve more than 3 turns from full open is impaired (Table C-2). A diesel tank under an 8-hour supply is impaired on that table. Lock valves only when they are wide open and restored. Do not lock a closed valve (§3.2).

Hot work is prohibited in an unprotected area (FM DS 10-7 §2.2.2.4). An impairment does not run through the post-work watch and the monitoring tail (FM DS 10-3 §2.5.2.1.3).

NFPA 25-2026 §15.5.2(4), in the pull, treats an outage longer than 10 hours in a 24-hour period as requiring evacuation, an approved fire watch, a temporary water supply, or an approved program that controls ignition. Confirm the licensed text. The section number stays `[V]` with the other NFPA 25 cites in `itm-testability.md`.

Planned impairments often need notice measured in business days under a local fire rule. Confirm the adopted rule. Do not copy an 8-hour threshold from another city.

The owner authorizes the impairment. A contractor impairment is allowed only when it is communicated before the work, supervised by the owner's responsible person, and confirmed restored (FM DS 10-4, January 2024, §2.2.4.5 and §2.2.4.5.1). The same sheet defaults hot-work permits to that owner employee (§2.2.4.6). Contractor training is refreshed at least every 3 years, and a refresher is recommended after 12 months off site (§2.2.3.2). Keep training records at least 3 years (§2.2.3.4.1). Keep basis documents, as-builts, and manuals for the life of the equipment (§2.2.4.8).

## Hot work

FM DS 10-3 (October 2016 / interim July 2018) §2.5.2.2.1 sets the hot-work area at 35 ft horizontal and 15 ft vertical. Extend it to 50 ft horizontal in a draft, and to 35 ft vertical for torch cutting, grinding, or arc welding.

The default tail is 1 hour of fire watch plus 3 hours of monitoring (§2.5.4.3). The worst case in Table 1 is 1 hour plus 5 hours. Torch-applied roofing is 2 hours of watch plus 2 hours of monitoring, or 1 hour plus 1 hour if an infrared camera is used (Table 1, note 1). Patrol at least every 15 minutes when automatic smoke detection cannot be used (§2.5.4.2). Stop work above 1 percent of the lower explosive limit (§2.5.2.2.5). Remove charred roofing above 250 °F out to a 4 ft radius (§2.5.2.2.11). A permit covers one shift (§2.5.1.1).

Ignitable liquids are removed, or continuous vapor detection is provided, during hot work near them. The carrier sheet that states the removal does not state a percent-of-limit number. Use the 1 percent FM stop unless a stricter number is in force.
