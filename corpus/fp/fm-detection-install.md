# FM detection installation (DS 5-48)

Cite FM DS 5-48 as January 2011 with the April 2025 interim. The April 2025 interim moved duct rules out of an obsolete sheet into §2.2.3. White-space port density and the 60 second transport stay on FM DS 5-32. This sheet's own air-sampling transport of 120 seconds, and an area approaching 20,000 ft², do not raise the hall limit (§2.2.3.3.13).

Table 4 places 900 ft² and 30 ft smoke spacing at 6 air changes or fewer. That breakpoint is stricter than the NFPA 7.5 air-change row (`../fa/asd-performance.md`). A hall at 78 to 265 air changes is past the last row (125 ft² at 60 air changes). Do not invent a denser row.

Do not use spot smoke above 100 °F, above 93 percent relative humidity, or above 300 ft/min unless the detector is FM Approved for those conditions (§2.2.3.1.3). A raised floor and a suspended ceiling are separate rooms (§2.2.3.1.2). Spot smoke and heat stay at least 4 percent of the ceiling height off the wall (§2.2.2.2.1 and §2.2.3.2.1). Beam detectors at a lower elevation are spaced at no more than 30 percent of the installation height (§2.2.3.3.5). Keep smoke at least 3 ft from a weak supply and 9 ft from a strong supply. A duct detector is at least 6 duct widths from a diffuser or a bend (§2.2.3.3.10).

FM Approved smoke responds before 4 percent per foot (Appendix C.3.6).

This sheet's preaction detection default is heat, one zone, and no cross-zone, unless the occupancy sheet requires smoke (§2.2.1.14.1 and §2.2.1.14.3). A data hall on DS 5-32 requires the early-warning smoke layout, so the heat default does not govern the hall. On a non-smooth ceiling, heat spacing does not exceed the greater of half the approved linear spacing and the full sprinkler spacing (§2.2.1.14.6). Water demand follows that spacing: wet when heat detectors are within the maximum, and dry when they exceed it, when the space is refrigerated at full approved spacing, or when pilot sprinklers are used (§2.2.1.14.7). Smooth-ceiling labels in Table 2 run from 10 ft by 10 ft through 15, 20, 25, and 30 ft squares to 35 ft by 35 ft.

Pilot operation to valve open is 40 seconds or less (§2.2.7.2). A heat-detector functional test responds within 1 minute (Table 7). Sensitivity is tested at 1 year, then every 2 years, and may extend to 5 years after two successive passes (§2.3.2.4.1). Nonrestorable heat is sampled at 2 per 100 units at 15 years (Table 7 and §2.3.2.3.1). A stand-alone detector has at least 24 hours of backup (§2.2.1.2).

Test air sampling from the end port and confirm airflow at the other ports. Disconnect solenoids or close valves before any test that could release (§2.3.1.1).

Electrical rooms on an FM job: spot photoelectric or combination smoke to a constantly attended location (FM DS 5-19 §2.2.4), spaced per this sheet. Add fixed-temperature heat in a high-ambient electrical wiring room (§2.2.1.5). Keep the heads out of arc-flash vent paths.
