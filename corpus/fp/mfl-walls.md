# Loss-limiting walls and space separation

What the scenario means, including the impaired-sprinkler assumption, is `mfl-evaluation.md`. Ordinary exposure with sprinklers credited is `campus-exterior-exposure.md`. This note is the construction. The sheet is FM DS 1-42, April 2019, interim April 2025.

Attached sections use a loss-limiting fire wall. Detached buildings use space separation. The default wall is 4-hour, non-loadbearing, reinforced masonry or concrete (§2.2.1.1). Gypsum on studs, exterior insulation finish systems, cellular plastic insulation, and mass timber are not this wall. Each leaf of a double wall is 3 hours (§2.2.10.3).

Stability is part of the rating. Lateral load is at least 5 psf allowable or 8 psf strength (§2.2.2.7). Table 1 clearance at a 40 ft bay is 5 in, and at a 60 ft bay is 7.5 in. Cantilever walls are avoided in the sheet's 50- to 500-year earthquake zones. Prefer double walls at rack-supported structures.

Penetrations are avoided. Where one remains: an FM Approved firestop and an approved installer, independent sprinkler supplies on each side, and no ignitable-liquid or combustible-gas pipe. Ducts and conduit are noncombustible. Heating, ventilating, and air-conditioning crossings use slip or breakaway joints.

Space separation is the base distance times the factors the sheet names, written SM = SB × U × M (§2.3.1.2). The general minimum is 10 ft (§2.3.1.1). Table 3 can show no extra separation once the exposed wall is 4 hours, beyond structural separation and Table 1. Explosion, boiling-liquid expanding-vapor, and runaway occupancies use 125 ft (§2.2.2.10 and §2.3.1.1). Ignitable liquid at or above 500 gal uses the ignitable-liquid figure path (§2.3.4). Exposing length used in the charts is at most 500 ft (§2.3.5.1).

The roof strip is a Class 1 deck and a Class A cover for 50 ft on both sides of the wall, with vents and rooftop units at least 50 ft away (§2.2.4). The parapet is at least 30 in unless an elevation difference the sheet allows omits it (§2.2.4.1).

Openings are minimized and protected on both sides. Doors are FM Approved 3-hour, without glazing (§2.5.5.1). Clear space in front is at least the larger opening dimension and at least 6 ft (§2.5.5.4). A personnel opening is at most 4 ft by 8 ft and normally closed (§2.5.6.1). A double wall that needs a pair of opposite-swing doors uses a 4-hour reinforced-concrete vestibule designed for 100 psf (§2.5.6). Water spray and water curtains are not the opening protection (§2.5.3.1). Detectors follow DS 5-48. A waterflow switch alone does not trip the door.

A duct through the wall gets two 3-hour dampers and slip joints. Closure is by 135 °F or 165 °F detectors, or ambient plus 50 °F (§2.5.10.2). Combustible pneumatic tubing does not cross the wall. Door closers fail safe on loss of power. Close speed is 6 in/s to 24 in/s. Trip-test semiannually without cutting the fusible link (§2.5.11).

Multiple water supplies limit the loss area only when they share no main, transformer, or interconnect. That credit needs the insurer's engineer (§2.6). In the sheet's earthquake zones the wall is an essential facility, importance 1.5 (Appendix C).

An interior 2-hour fire barrier is a different wall (`compartmentation.md`). The schedule method `fire-area-compartmentation-schedule` does not size this sheet.
