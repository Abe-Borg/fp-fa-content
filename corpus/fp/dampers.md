# Fire and smoke dampers

Which wall is rated is `compartmentation.md`. In-duct sprinklers are `exhaust-ducts.md`. Duct detectors and the area-detection waiver are `duct-smoke.md`. This note is the damper as an opening protective.

A static fire damper is listed to close in a system that shuts down on fire. A dynamic fire damper is listed to close while air is moving. In a data hall the fans often stay running: the return-detector waiver in the mechanical code removes the device that would have shut them down, and a duct detector is supervisory (`duct-smoke.md`). Specify dynamic fire dampers, or combination fire-and-smoke dampers, on air paths that are not proven to shut down. Dynamic is an acceptable selection even where the fans do shut down.

Oversized dynamic openings cannot be broken up with support mullions. Those mullions are a static-damper device. The largest multi-section size on the chosen model's listing is the largest opening through the rated wall. If no multi-section size is listed, the model is single-section only. Change the architectural opening when the listing is smaller. Check category EMME in the directory at design development.

A fire barrier under 3 hours takes a 1½-hour damper. An assembly of 3 hours or more takes a 3-hour damper. A 2-hour hall wall is the 1½-hour damper. The vendor manual this digest uses was written to IBC 2015 §717.3.2. Confirm the adopted edition's table. The split is the same one `compartmentation.md` carries.

IBC requires smoke-damper leakage of at least Class II. Class I is available from more than one manufacturer and is the tighter selection for a pressurization boundary. Elevated-temperature rating: the code floor cited in that manual is 250 °F, and 350 °F is widely available. Modulating actuators and extruded-aluminum blades in that manual stop at 250 °F. A schedule that asks for 350 °F and a modulating actuator contradicts itself.

Blade style has a velocity cap. A common 3V blade in that manual stops at 2,000 ft/min. Large hall ducts exceed that. Higher velocity also shrinks the damper one actuator can close.

Actuators are factory mounted under the editions of UL 555 and UL 555S the manual cites. A field-substituted actuator voids the listing. Sleeve length is on the order of the damper plus the wall plus the angles. Standard sleeves in that manual are 16 in, 21 in, and 24 in. Provide an access door at the damper. External actuator mounting is the default. All ducts terminate at the sleeve.

The listed expansion gap is about 1/8 in per foot of damper width or height, at least 1/4 in and at most 1½ in. Packing that gap as a firestop, or caulking the retaining angles, can void the damper listing. A manufacturer permission for a named sealant is a written submittal item, not a field habit (`firestops.md`).

A smoke damper may sit in the duct with its centerline within 24 in of the smoke barrier. A combination damper is in the rated barrier, in a sleeve, unless the model is listed for out-of-wall installation.

Two 3-hour dampers at a loss-limiting wall are `mfl-walls.md`. That is a different wall.

The interface method is `fa-hvac-door-and-lock-interfaces`. The barrier schedule is `fire-area-compartmentation-schedule`.
