# Cold-climate freeze program

Screen the site on the FM freeze map. Do not invent a 100-year design minimum temperature from a figure in a PDF. FM DS 9-18 (July 2022) applies when that temperature is 20 °F or lower (§1.0, §3.1). The same threshold is the freeze-plan trigger in `preincident-environment.md`. Heat-trace listing and supervision are `heat-trace-supervision.md` and `freeze-heat-trace.md`. Tank heaters are `water-tanks.md`.

## Campus package

Water-filled spaces, including concealed spaces at exterior walls, stay above 40 °F (DS 9-18 §2.2.1). Prefer not to run wet pipe in an exterior wall (§2.2.3). An electric pump room stays above 40 °F. A diesel pump room stays above 70 °F (§2.2.4). NFPA 20-2022 and 2025 §4.14.3.1 require the pump room above 40 °F. For a diesel engine, §11.6.5.1 requires the engine manufacturer's minimum. The 70 °F diesel floor is the FM sheet, not NFPA 20. Put the room heat on generator-backed power so it survives the outage the sheet plans for.

Exterior risers, including dry and double-interlock valves, sit in an insulated heated enclosure with temperature reported to a constantly attended location (§2.2.6). Heat trace does not replace that enclosure.

If any portion cannot hold 40 °F, use dry pipe or double-interlock as the occupancy sheet allows (§2.2.7). Freeze is a second reason for that election. It does not replace it. Antifreeze is not the company default.

Private mains are buried at least 6 in. deeper than the municipal main, because they do not circulate (FM DS 3-10 §2.2.15.1). Confirm the local frost depth. Do not rely on a map figure alone (`private-fire-mains.md`).

Low-temperature alarms and leak detection go to a constantly attended location (§2.3.1). Plan for at least 3 days without electricity or gas (§2.4.1), with emergency fuel for that long (§2.4.5). Idle buildings stay at or above 40 °F or are converted to a dry system with the sprinklers still in service (§2.6.2). Do not shut the system off to avoid a freeze. Insulation without flow does not prevent freezing (§3.3).

Instrument air is dried to a dew point 18 °F below the map temperature (§2.4.6). That is the air supply, not a sprinkler C-factor.

## Tanks

DS 9-18 does not size tank heaters. FM DS 3-2 (October 2015 / interim July 2022) does. Hold the coldest water at or above 42 °F and alarm at 40 °F (§2.2.6). The same two numbers are NFPA 22-2018 and 2023 §16.1.2 and §16.1.2.2. NFPA 22 still keys the heating decision to the lowest one-day mean temperature from an approved source (§16.1.2.1). That is a different metric from the FM 100-year map. Do not convert one into the other. Heat a suction tank when the FM zone is −5 °F or colder (DS 3-2 §2.2.6.5). An air bubbler is not a fire-protection tank heater (§3.8.5).

A sheet example for an uninsulated 100,000 gallon suction tank in a −20 °F zone is 556,000 Btu/h, and at 20 psi steam that is about 23 ft² of coil. Use it to see the path: map zone, then Tables 6 through 9, then Table 12, then Table 13. Do not reuse the zone or the Btu figure on another site.
