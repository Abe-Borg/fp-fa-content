# Density, area, and the dry / double-interlock increase

On an FM HC-2 white-space hall the usual controlling density-method demand in this pack is 0.2 gpm/ft² over 2,500 ft² wet or 3,500 ft² dry or double-interlock, plus 250 gpm hose and 60 minutes. That is 750 gpm and 45,000 gallons wet, or 950 gpm and 57,000 gallons dry or double-interlock. Confirm the sheet edition (FM DS 5-32 January 2026 for the data-center occupancy; demand backbone FM DS 3-26 April 2025). Do not copy those figures onto a hall that is not that hazard.

FM states absolute areas. NFPA 13 states a relative increase. Do not stack them. Never run a controlling area of 3,500 ft² and also apply a 30 percent increase.

The code-floor increase for dry pipe and double-interlock is 30 percent:

- NFPA 13-2025 §19.2.3.2.6
- NFPA 13-2022 §19.2.3.2.5
- NFPA 13-2019 §19.3.3.2.5

NFPA 13-2025 §19.2.3.2.5 is a different rule (ceilings over 30 ft). Always name the edition with that section number.

Code-floor density/area examples held for new systems, NFPA 13-2025 Table 19.2.3.1.1 (same table number in 2022 for new systems): Ordinary Group 1 is 0.15 gpm/ft² over 1,500 ft²; Ordinary Group 2 is 0.20 gpm/ft² over 1,500 ft². Apply the 30 percent increase to the area, then print the delta against the FM absolute area. Do not invent a third percentage.

Hose and duration, NFPA 13-2025 Table 19.2.3.1.2: ordinary hazard 250 gpm for 60 or 90 minutes; extra hazard 500 gpm for 90 or 120 minutes. The lower duration needs supervision per §19.2.3.1.3. Print that row next to the FM 250 gpm / 60 minute row and keep the more stringent one that actually applies.

Li-ion energy-storage rooms are not HC-2. IFC 2024 §1207.5.5 item 1 and FM DS 5-33 §2.4.1 use 0.30 gpm/ft² over the room or 2,500 ft², whichever is smaller. The dry / double-interlock 30 percent increase on a 2,500 ft² cap is 3,250 ft² and 975 gpm before hose. Whether the area may be cut back to a smaller room is `[V]` with the engineer of record. NFPA 13-2025 does not say what to do when the adjusted area exceeds the room except for the small-room rules in §19.2.3.1.4 and §19.2.3.3.4.
