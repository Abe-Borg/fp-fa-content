# Freeze, flood, and pre-incident planning

FM DS 10-1 (July 2021 / interim January 2023) §2.2.1.1.2 triggers a freeze plan at a 100-year design minimum temperature of 20 °F or lower. The same section triggers a flood plan in a 500-year flood zone and a snow plan when the ground snow load is at least 5 psf.

Occupied wet-pipe spaces stay at or above 40 °F. Diesel fire-pump rooms stay at or above 70 °F. Plan for at least 3 days of utility loss (§2.2.5). The freeze plan includes a daily 2 in. drain check during freezing weather. Heat-trace limits and the listing fence are `freeze-heat-trace.md`. Heat tracing does not replace a heated valve enclosure where FM DS 9-18 requires the enclosure.

An unknown roof snow load is estimated as the larger of 0.65 times the flat-roof snow load and 15 psf. Act when the load is near 50 percent of live-load capacity (§2.2.7).

Wind shutters are completable in one 8-hour shift, with the hardware installed in advance (§2.2.9).

The response team in Table 2.2.3.5 has a leader, a notifier, a sprinkler-valve operator, and a fire-pump operator where a pump is provided.

Audit the plan at least annually, and train on each peril before its season (§2.2.1.4).

These are planning triggers. They do not change density, area, or the double-interlock sequence.
