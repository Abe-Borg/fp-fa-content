# Seismic shutoff of fuel gas and ignitable liquid

Sprinkler bracing is `seismic-bracing.md`. This note is fuel piping and shutoff after an earthquake.

FM DS 1-11 (July 2016 / interim April 2025) applies in the FM 50-year through 500-year zones and floors equipment force at 0.50 times weight at the center of gravity (§2.3.1.2).

Sway braces on fuel-gas and ignitable-liquid piping are 30 ft apart transversely for 1 in. and 2 in. pipe and 40 ft for 2-1/2 in. and larger, with longitudinal braces at 80 ft (Table 2.3.4.4A). The horizontal factor on that fuel piping is 0.75, 0.5, and 0.4 for the 50-year, 100-year, and 250-to-500-year zones (Table 3.2.2.2). Those factors are not the sprinkler G from DS 2-8. Do not swap them.

A seismic joint is sized as 1.5 percent of height each way, 3 percent total (§2.3.4.1). Equipment gets at least 6 in. of slack (§2.3.3.1).

A seismic gas shutoff closes in at most 5 seconds, fail-closed, and is mounted within 3 ft of grade (§2.3.5.2.2 and §2.3.5.3.1). Ignitable liquids with a flash point below 100 °F get a remote shutoff (§2.3.6.2). The sheet excludes seismic shutoff on hydrogen and on lube oil.

ASCE 25-2006, as cited in §3.2.2.1, actuates at 0.70 g in 0.13 seconds, 0.40 g in 0.2 seconds, 0.30 g in 0.4 seconds, or 0.25 g in 1.0 second.

Inspect these restraints yearly and test them about every 5 to 7 years, on normal and emergency power for remote systems (§2.4.1).
