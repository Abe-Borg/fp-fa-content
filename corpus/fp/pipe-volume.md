# Trapped water volume

Volume for a dry or double-interlock zone is the sum of pipe segments. Wet-pipe volume is informational only. This note does not prove delivery time and does not select a nitrogen generator.

Gallons for one segment: (pi/4) × (inside diameter in inches)^2 / 144 × 7.48 × length in feet. Inside diameter is outside diameter minus twice the wall. Manufacturer inside diameter governs. NFPA 13-2025 Table A.8.2.4 matches NFPA 13-2022 Table A.8.2.3 cell for cell in the pack's cross-check (3/4 in. Schedule 10 is blank in both). Do not treat that cross-check as a license to paste the table. Look up the diameter you are using.

Fitting allowance:

- Default 0 percent when lengths are center-to-center through fittings. The fitting body is already in the length. Grooved fittings add no net volume on that basis.
- Equivalent length used for friction is not volume. Do not add it to the gallons.

Report per segment, per zone, the largest single zone, and the connected total in each riser room. The largest zone feeds the 30-minute fill. The connected total feeds generator maintenance. See `nitrogen-compressor-and-c-factor.md`.

Flags, not exemptions by themselves: double-interlock cap 500 gallons (NFPA 13 §8.3.2.3.1.1, 2019 / 2022 / 2025); dry-pipe quick-opening path 750 gallons (NFPA 13-2025 §8.2.4.2.2). The delivery screen decides whether a cap is actually available.
