# Exterior fire exposure and wildland

Transformer apparatus spacing is `transformer-siting.md`. A loss scenario that assumes sprinklers are impaired is `mfl-evaluation.md` and `mfl-walls.md`. This note is exposure while active protection is credited, plus wildland. Building and yard exposure is FM DS 1-20, April 2025. Vegetation is FM DS 9-19, January 2022, interim January 2024. DS 1-20 excludes wildland. A campus often needs both.

Building-to-building preference (§2.2.4 of DS 1-20): sprinkle the exposing building or yard, then separate to DS 1-42, then upgrade the exposed wall and its openings. A separation shorter than DS 1-42 needs the insurer's engineer and a credible fire-department response. Prefer the first two when the campus owns both sides. Older category tables in pre-2012 editions of DS 1-20 are retired. Numeric separation is DS 1-42 (`mfl-walls.md`) or the occupancy sheet. Do not invent a transformer standoff from an exposure-sprinkler table.

Dumpsters stay at least 30 ft from an exposed building unless the wall is blank, at least 1 hour, and free of combustible eaves. Loaded trailers count as yard storage.

Wildland exposure exists within 0.5 mile of forest or woodland, or within 200 ft of brush or grassland (DS 9-19 §1.0). The ember package at those distances is noncombustible walls, a Class A roof, mesh at or below 1/8 in, and a 5 ft vegetation-free zone, even before a radiant calculation trips. When radiation exceeds what the passive wall can take, the preference is a reduced-fuel zone to the distances in Tables 2.3.2.A and 2.3.2.B, then a wall upgrade to DS 1-20, then exterior sprinklers (§2.3.3).

Exterior sprinklers for a building or yard use DS 1-20 Table 2.4.1. Wildland vegetation uses DS 9-19 Table 2.5.1, which is binned by heat flux. The hardware family is an FM nonstorage vertical sidewall, quick-response, 165 °F, 6 in to 12 in off the wall, installed to DS 2-0. Those tables are campus demand. They are not an interior ordinary-hazard calculation. DS 1-20 duration language is 60 minutes where that sheet applies. DS 9-19 does not restate it. Confirm wildland duration with the insurer's engineer.

DS 9-19 rounds the noncombustible radiant trip to 30 kW/m² for wildland. The DS 1-22 glossary figure of 27 kW/m² stays on the loss scenario (`mfl-evaluation.md`). The round-up is intentional on the wildland sheet.

Interior hall sprinklers do not protect a combustible roof from exterior radiation or embers. Ground-array setbacks use this note for the building and the vegetation, and `ground-mounted-pv.md` for the array itself.

The loop and hydrant method for the added flow is `campus-looped-main-sizing`. The interior barrier method is `fire-area-compartmentation-schedule`.
