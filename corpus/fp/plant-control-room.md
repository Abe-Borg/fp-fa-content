# Plant control and building-management rooms

A building-management, energy-management, or industrial-control room on the campus is not a data hall. FM DS 5-32 still owns an associated data hall, and it wants a process-hazard review before an automatic shutdown of that hall (§2.3.10). This room's construction and detection are FM DS 7-110, June 2026. Chemical-plant process sheets are out of this note.

Construction: noncombustible room and panel construction (§2.2.4 and §2.2.11). At least 1 hour of separation from adjacent areas, including low-voltage switchgear (§2.2.5). Redundant control trains sit in separate fire-rated areas (§2.2.6). Liquid lines are routed away or contained, with leak detection to a constantly attended location (§2.2.9). Elevated supports that are fire-exposed get at least 1 hour of fireproofing (§2.2.3). The 1-hour line is an insurer room rating. It is not the hall-to-hall fire barrier in `compartmentation.md`.

Detection is FM Approved smoke to a constantly attended location (§2.3.2). Business-critical and safety systems add very-early-warning detection in the room or inside the panel (§2.3.3). Sample below a raised floor and above a cable ceiling (§2.3.4). Install to DS 5-48. A remote room still alarms to a constantly attended location.

Suppression follows construction. A combustible room uses wet pipe or double-interlock preaction with quick-response sprinklers per DS 3-26 (§2.3.6.1). Hazard category 1 applies to a control room or center whose ceiling is at most 30 ft. Hazard category 2 applies to an instrumentation equipment room, or to any of these rooms above 30 ft. The densities, hose, and duration live in DS 3-26. This sheet does not print them. Water mist on the sheet is out of scope.

A noncombustible room is where that sheet's preferred agent is out of scope (§2.3.6.2). Use wet pipe or double-interlock preaction. Any automatic power-down still needs the process-hazard review in §2.3.6.2 and §2.3.10. Power isolation for information-technology equipment is `hall-power-isolation.md`.

Dry-chemical extinguishers are refused on electronics (§2.3.13). The pre-incident plan brings electrical staff and the fire service together for de-energizing (§2.3.14).

The method for the three-layer choice is `most-stringent-basis-matrix`. Detection spacing, once the room is classified, is `fa-spot-smoke-spacing-airflow-and-beams`.
