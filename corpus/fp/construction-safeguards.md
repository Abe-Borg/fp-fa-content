# Protection during construction and demolition

FM DS 1-0 (interim July 2023) §2.3.3.1 activates sprinklers and standpipes as soon as the shell can hold them, before combustibles enter. Extend capped standpipes with hose outlets as floors rise, and tell the fire department when they are in service (§2.3.3.2).

Combustible temporary buildings are at least 50 ft from the main building and 30 ft apart, or they are sprinklered (§2.3.1.1).

Only FM Approved 4652 debris barriers go over live sprinklers. Deflectors are 1.5 ft to 10 ft above the barrier, and the barrier is at least 8 ft above the floor (§2.3.3.4). That barrier is limited to hazard categories 1 and 2, a roof slope of at most 10 degrees, and a duration under 180 days.

Yard piles are at most 6 ft high, with 20 ft, 25 ft, or 30 ft separation at pile widths of 4 ft, 6 ft, or 8 ft (§2.3.5.5).

Watch rounds are every half hour for 2 hours after work stops, then hourly (§3.1.11).

Site permanent work above the 500-year flood, plus 1 ft to 2 ft of freeboard (§2.2.1.1).

During demolition, keep sprinklers in service until the combustible need ends. Take them out of service section by section, and verify the control valve open at the end of each workday (§2.4.2.1).

These are construction safeguards for wet, dry, and double-interlock systems. They do not change the basis of design.
