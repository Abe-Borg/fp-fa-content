# ITM designed at construction documents

Inspection, testing, and maintenance is a design input. Two choices made on the drawings permanently change the insurer's own cadence. Neither can be bought later, and both exist only when FM grants them on company-elected wet, dry, or double-interlock. They do not apply to a single-interlock basis of design.

1. Nitrogen in the dry or double-interlock system, installed that way from the start: FM DS 2-81 Table 2c item 14 moves the full-flow trip test from every 3 years to every 10 years. NFPA 25 still calls for a full-open trip every 3 years (preaction §13.4.3.2.2, partial-open in other years §13.4.3.2.3; dry §13.4.5.2.2.2, with an annual trip at §13.4.5.2.2). The pack's NFPA 25 reading covers 2017 through 2026 as the insurer's characterization. Licensed NFPA 25 text is not in the codebook, so every NFPA 25 frequency is `[V]` until the licensed book is checked. Do not invent a 2026 section number.
2. FM Approved enhanced-security valve supervision: Table 1 items 1a and 1c move the control-valve visual inspection from weekly to semiannually.

Per-parameter most-stringent still applies. A weekly code floor is not waived by an insurer lever the project did not actually buy.

## What the drawings have to show

Provide a test header, a drain of at least 4 in. where the main size requires it, a backflow-preventer test path, zoning that can be impaired one zone at a time, and alarm isolation so a test does not false-alarm the station. Write the 30 second remote-end criterion into the specification when FM DS 5-32 applies. The delivery calc itself is not this note.

Combined test-and-drain hardware is a product family. Cite the family from the brochure in hand. Do not invent model numbers. A pressure-reducing valve with a hydro-lockout trim is only in scope when the wet basis of design and the opened sheet support it. Reconcile the PRV section to the enforced NFPA 13 (pack cite: NFPA 13-2022 and 2025 §16.9.7).

NFPA 4 integrated-system testing and the commissioning packet are the acceptance program (`commissioning-ist-acceptance-packet`). They are not the 25-year calendar. Do not emit an operations manual from this note.

If a product line cannot be confirmed from a note that is actually in hand, mark that line `handoff` and finish the rest of the checklist.
