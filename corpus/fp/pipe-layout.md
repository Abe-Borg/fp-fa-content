# Pipe wall, pitch, hangers, and obstructions

FM DS 2-0 below is October 2021 with the January 2024 interim, unless a section says otherwise. Company scope is wet, dry, and double-interlock. C = 100 on dry and double-interlock is `nitrogen-compressor-and-c-factor.md`. The C = 120 column in DS 2-0 Table 2.2.1.3.3 is comparison-only.

## Wall and material

Data-center minimum wall thickness runs from 0.126 in. on 1 in. and 1-1/2 in. pipe to 0.330 in. on 12 in. pipe (Table 2.4.1.1.2.1). Leak-sensitive occupancies prefer Schedule 40 (FM DS 2-1 §2.2.1.3).

Plain air, without nitrogen, is acceptable only with a regenerative dryer or on a system of 20 sprinklers or fewer (§2.2.1.10.3). Pendent heads on preaction are allowed only where the area cannot freeze and the pipe is galvanized or kept on nitrogen (§2.2.4.3.2). Galvanized dry or preaction pipe can pinhole in 2 to 3 years from residual water (FM DS 2-1 §3.6). Take the nitrogen route when pendent heads need that permission. Avoid rolled grooves, and point the weld seam toward the roof, at least 45 degrees from the floor (FM DS 2-1 §2.2.1.12.2).

Black steel on dry or preaction is acceptable when nitrogen is used for the life of the system and the generator is FM Approved (FM DS 2-1, October 2016 / interim July 2022, §2.2.1.12.1). Sampling that proves the nitrogen arrived is `nitrogen-sampling.md`.

## Pitch, hangers, obstructions

Pitch dry or preaction branch lines at least 1/2 in. per 10 ft, and other pipe at least 1/4 in. per 10 ft, toward a 2 in. drain. Hanger spacing is 12 ft for 1 in. and 1-1/4 in. pipe and 15 ft for 1-1/2 in. and larger when the minimum wall is met.

An object 4 ft or less in its least dimension is not an obstruction on the nonstorage layout (§2.5.2.5.4). A wider object on preaction is a calculated supplement of at most four sprinklers (§2.5.2.5.5.3). Storage tightens the exemption to 2 ft (`storage-design.md`).

Standard-coverage minimums are 7 ft and 64 ft². Hazard category 2 maximum area is 130 ft² (Tables 2.5.2.3.1.1). Feed and cross mains may be looped in a nonstorage occupancy (§2.2.1.5.3).

Do not install gravity heat or smoke vents in a building protected by ceiling sprinklers (§2.5.2.1.3.1). If they remain, open them only on waterflow, and not before 10 minutes.

The fire-department connection is 4 in. for risers through 8 in. and 6 in. above that (§2.3.6.6). Water-supply flow-test data are no older than 12 months (§2.6.4.6). Local amendments often shorten that age or add a pressure cushion. Do not copy another city's cushion. Confirm the adopted rule.

Releasing-panel standby of 90 hours on DS 2-0 §2.2.1.12.1.3 matches the FM practice in `../fa/secondary-power.md`. Confirm the sheet revision before you treat it as current.
