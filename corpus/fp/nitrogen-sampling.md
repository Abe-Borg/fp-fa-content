# Nitrogen delivery, sampling, and the purity rule that is not a C-factor

Calculate dry and double-interlock pipe at C = 100. Do not chase C = 120. That lock, and the two fill capacities, are `nitrogen-compressor-and-c-factor.md`. This note is whether the nitrogen is actually in the pipe.

A 2024 instrumented test on a 500 gallon rig, read at 14 days, reached about 93.5 percent at a remote orifice, 86.3 percent on a continuous remote sample, and 82.8 percent at the riser purge. Every method still read at least 98 percent at the purge valve. At 8 weeks the best of those three methods was about 97.3 percent, with supply gas at 99.2 to 99.5 percent. A 14-day, 98 percent, system-wide claim was not met. Sample the remote branch ends. A high reading at a purge port does not prove the ends.

FM coupon tests found air corroding carbon steel on the order of 20 times faster than nitrogen on dry or preaction pipe, and up to about 14 times faster on a wet purge (FM DS 2-1 §3.2 and §3.3). A mix near 95 percent nitrogen tracked toward air. Microbiologically influenced corrosion was about 10 to 20 percent of the fire-protection corrosion cases FM reviewed. Chemical treatment is not the recommendation. Replace the pipe (FM DS 2-1 §3.8.5).

The 98 percent nitrogen and 1.5 psi per hour leakage rule is the increased-C program (NFPA 13-2022 §8.2.6.9.3; NFPA 13-2025 §8.2.10.2). It is not ordinary supervision, and it is not permission to calculate at C = 120.

Acceptance leak test: 40 psi for 24 hours, and correct a loss greater than 1-1/2 psi (NFPA 13-2019 §28.2.2.1; NFPA 13-2022 and 2025 §29.2.2.1). FM DS 2-81 §3.1.6.1 uses the same 1.5 psi in 24 hours at 40 psi.

Do not use liquid nitrogen. A small liquid release can drive a room under the 19.5 percent oxygen deficient-atmosphere limit in 29 CFR 1910.134(b). The generator does not replace the fill compressor.

NFPA 25-2026 has no nitrogen-specific trip interval in the pull. The full-flow trip comparison stays at 3 years in NFPA 25, with the FM 10-year lever only when FM grants it on a system that was nitrogen from the start (`itm-testability.md`). The purity check in the NFPA 25 reading is semiannual (§13.4.3.2.15 and §13.4.5.2.10). Those NFPA 25 section numbers stay `[V]` until the licensed book is checked, the same way the existing note treats them.

A listed generator's gallon cell at the supervisory pressure is a listing limit, not an NFPA cap. A zone above that cell needs a lower pressure, a split, or a separate fill compressor. Do not copy a model number from another hall.
