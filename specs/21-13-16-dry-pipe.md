# Section 21 13 16 — Dry-pipe sprinkler systems (draft)

Draft language. Verify with the engineer of record before issue. A figure the tools did not return is ASSUMED, or it is a cited screen. It is not a calculated zone result.

This section is dry pipe, CSI 21 13 16. Double-interlock preaction is Section 21 13 19. Do not specify a double-interlock valve, solenoid, or releasing matrix in this section.

## Part 1 — General

### 1.1 Summary

Provide dry pipe only where the suppression election for that space is dry pipe. Unheated exposures are the usual reason. A missing election is ASSUMED. It is not a silent swap to wet pipe or to double-interlock.

### 1.2 Design criteria

Decide each criterion by the most stringent of the code floor, the insurer sheet, and the owner standard. Cite the section and the edition. An unamended 2024 I-Code floor references NFPA 13-2022 until the adoption is verified. Do not cite NFPA 13-2025 as the code minimum unless that edition was adopted.

The dry and double-interlock code-floor area increase is 30 percent, and it is not stacked on an insurer absolute area. The cites are NFPA 13-2025 §19.2.3.2.6, NFPA 13-2022 §19.2.3.2.5, and NFPA 13-2019 §19.3.3.2.5. NFPA 13-2025 §19.2.3.2.5 is the ceiling-height rule, not the increase. Do not apply the increase twice because this section is dry pipe.

The dry-pipe quick-opening size path is dry pipe only: NFPA 13-2025 §8.2.4.2.2; NFPA 13-2022 §8.2.3.4 (750 gallons). Do not lend that path to a double-interlock zone.

A delivery time from the trapped-volume screen is an optimistic screen, not a listed water-delivery-time calculation. Where an insurer sheet sets 30 seconds to design pressure at the most remote sprinkler, first water at the inspector's test connection is not that endpoint. Do not blend the 60 second inspection route with the 30 second insurer clock.

Do not copy another hall's gallons.

## Part 2 — Products

### 2.1 Valve, air, and nitrogen

Specify the dry valve, accelerator or other quick-opening device, and supervisory devices by function and listing. Do not elect a catalog number. Quick-opening-device trip time is not water-delivery time.

Design Hazen-Williams C is 100. C = 120 is comparison-only and is not the design value.

Nitrogen, when the election includes it, is a corrosion decision, not a hydraulic credit. Liquid nitrogen is not the default. Do not freeze a compressor or generator model number. Maintenance capacity covers the connected gallons. The 30-minute fill is the largest single system (NFPA 13-2025 §8.2.7.3; 2019 and 2022 §8.2.6.3.2). Size the fill on the absolute basis. The sprinkler nitrogen generator is not the standpipe air compressor.

Black steel unless the owner standard requires galvanized for a named exposure. No CPVC in this section. Schedule and joining are ASSUMED until that standard is in hand: Schedule 40 at 2 in. and smaller, Schedule 10 roll-grooved at 2-1/2 in. and larger.

## Part 3 — Execution

### 3.1 Installation and acceptance

Provide low-point drains and an inspector's test at the remote end used for the delivery criterion that applies. Pitch pipe so it drains. Do not heat-trace drained dry pipe. Antifreeze is not the default in place of a dry system.

Hydrostatic test per the adopted NFPA 13: 200 psi for 2 hours, or 50 psi above system working pressure where that pressure is over 150 psi (NFPA 13-2025 and 2022 §29.2.1.1 and §29.2.1.3).

Supervision and alarm monitoring are specified in Section 28 31 00. Do not specify the fire-alarm panel here. Water on a dry valve is not the double-interlock cause and effect. Do not describe dry-pipe operation as detection plus low air.
