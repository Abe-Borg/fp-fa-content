# Section 21 13 19 — Double-interlock preaction (draft)

Draft language. Verify with the engineer of record before issue. The zone tool plan governs gallons and seconds. A delivery time from that screen is an optimistic screen, not a listed water-delivery-time calculation. A figure the tools did not return is ASSUMED.

This section is CSI 21 13 19. Do not write double-interlock requirements into Section 21 13 16. That number is dry pipe.

Single-interlock and non-interlock preaction are comparison-only. If either is the requested basis of design, stop and return an RFI. Do not retitle that system as this section, and do not file it under the dry-pipe number.

## Part 1 — General

### 1.1 Summary

Provide double-interlock preaction only where that space is elected double-interlock. A missing election is ASSUMED. It is not a silent swap to wet or dry. Coordinate releasing with Section 28 31 00. Do not specify the panel program here.

### 1.2 Design criteria

Decide each criterion by the most stringent of the code floor, the insurer sheet, and the owner standard. Cite the section and the edition. An unamended 2024 I-Code floor references NFPA 13-2022 until the adoption is verified. NFPA 13-2025 is a delta, not a silent minimum.

Do not stack the dry and double-interlock area increase on an insurer absolute area. The code-floor increase is 30 percent: NFPA 13-2025 §19.2.3.2.6, NFPA 13-2022 §19.2.3.2.5, NFPA 13-2019 §19.3.3.2.5. NFPA 13-2025 §19.2.3.2.5 is the ceiling-height rule, not that increase.

Where FM DS 5-32 applies, keep the data-hall zone at or under 500 gallons and at or under 30 seconds to design pressure at the single most remote sprinkler. Thirty seconds is that insurer criterion, not the NFPA floor. First water at the inspector's test connection is not that endpoint. The sheet does not authorize a single-interlock basis of design.

NFPA 13 §8.3.2.3.1.1 (2019, 2022, and 2025): at most 500 gallons, or 60 seconds, or a listed calculation. There is no 750 gallon path in that section. A quick-opening device does not raise the 500 gallon cap.

## Part 2 — Products

### 2.1 Valve and trim

Specify the valve, solenoid, and panel by function and listing. Do not elect a catalog number. Pick one opened trim sheet. Do not stack an electric/pneumatic actuator band on an electric/electric low-air switch.

Minimum supervisory air or nitrogen in the pack is 7 psi (NFPA 13-2025 and 2022 §8.3.2.5.2). That minimum is not the setpoint. Use the band on the elected sheet.

### 2.2 Pipe, nitrogen, and compressor

Black steel unless the owner standard requires galvanized for a named exposure. No CPVC in this section. Schedule and joining are ASSUMED until that standard is in hand: Schedule 40 at 2 in. and smaller, Schedule 10 roll-grooved at 2-1/2 in. and larger.

Design Hazen-Williams C is 100. C = 120 is comparison-only and understates friction. Do not size the system at 120.

Nitrogen is a corrosion decision, not a hydraulic credit. Liquid nitrogen is not the default. Do not freeze a generator model number. Maintenance capacity covers the connected gallons. The 30-minute fill (NFPA 13-2025 §8.2.7.3; 2019 and 2022 §8.2.6.3.2) is the largest single system, sized on the absolute basis. A listed compressor table governs when it is in hand.

## Part 3 — Execution

### 3.1 Installation and acceptance

Provide low-point drains. Do not heat-trace drained double-interlock pipe. A valve enclosure that must stay above freezing is a heated enclosure. Antifreeze is not a substitute.

Hydrostatic test per the adopted NFPA 13: 200 psi for 2 hours, or 50 psi above system working pressure where that pressure is over 150 psi (NFPA 13-2025 and 2022 §29.2.1.1 and §29.2.1.3). The code water-delivery test opens detection and the inspector's test together, not detection first.

Water opens only when the detection leg and the low-air leg are both true. Do not add a foam column or a clean-agent column. Record drawings match the program that was loaded.
