# Section 21 13 13 — Wet-pipe sprinkler systems (draft)

Draft language. Verify with the engineer of record before issue. A figure the tools did not return is ASSUMED. It is not a calculated result.

## Part 1 — General

### 1.1 Summary

Provide wet pipe only where the suppression election for that space is wet pipe. Support spaces are the usual use. A missing election is ASSUMED. It is not a silent swap to dry pipe or to double-interlock.

Spaces elected double-interlock belong in Section 21 13 19, not in this section. Do not write double-interlock requirements into Section 21 13 16.

### 1.2 Design criteria

Decide density, area, hose, and duration by the most stringent of the code floor, the insurer sheet, and the owner standard. Cite the section and the edition. An unamended 2024 I-Code floor references NFPA 13-2022 until the adoption is verified.

Code-floor examples for new systems, held for NFPA 13-2025 Table 19.2.3.1.1 and the same table number in 2022: Ordinary Group 1 is 0.15 gpm/ft² over 1,500 ft²; Ordinary Group 2 is 0.20 gpm/ft² over 1,500 ft². Light-hazard administrative areas are not the data-hall demand. An insurer absolute area is not increased again by 30 percent. Do not copy another hall's density onto a different hazard.

Hydraulic calculations use a water-supply test that is in hand. Test age is not frozen in this pack. Do not leave a blank in the issued section for static pressure, residual pressure, flow, date, or location. Ask for them.

Sprinkler response, temperature rating, K-factor, and finish follow the elected hazard and the listing. A quick-response sprinkler in a finished light-hazard room is a draft default only, and it is ASSUMED until the hazard election is recorded. Do not freeze a K-factor from another hall.

## Part 2 — Products

### 2.1 Pipe, sprinklers, and valves

Black steel unless the owner standard requires another material for a named exposure. No CPVC in this section. Schedule and joining are ASSUMED until that standard is in hand: Schedule 40 at 2 in. and smaller, Schedule 10 roll-grooved at 2-1/2 in. and larger.

Provide listed sprinklers, valves, and supervisory devices compatible with a wet system. Corrosion-resistant sprinklers only where the exposure requires them. Do not elect a catalog number.

Listed indicating control valves with tamper switches, and waterflow switches zoned the way the drawings show. Alarm and supervision are specified in Section 28 31 00. Do not specify the fire-alarm panel in this section.

## Part 3 — Execution

### 3.1 Installation, test, and closeout

Wet pipe operates by thermal opening of the sprinkler. Do not attach a double-interlock releasing program to a wet valve.

Provide a main drain at each riser and an inspector's test for each zone, discharging where water can be accepted. Combination test-and-drain hardware is a product family. Cite the family from the sheet in hand. Do not invent a model number.

Heat trace is only for wet piping that must stay wet and cannot be drained. It is not a heated valve house, and it is not for drained dry or double-interlock pipe. Do not invent a cable output or a controller setpoint. If the design temperature is missing, label it ASSUMED. Alarm a heat-trace failure to the fire-alarm panel as a supervisory signal when this section calls for the trace. Do not copy a catalog number from a prior retest. Antifreeze is not the default.

Hydrostatic test per the adopted NFPA 13: 200 psi for 2 hours, or 50 psi above system working pressure where that pressure is over 150 psi (NFPA 13-2025 and 2022 §29.2.1.1 and §29.2.1.3). Flush underground piping before connection, under Section 21 11 00.

Submit working plans and hydraulic calculations before fabrication. Closeout includes the contractor's material and test certificate, spare sprinklers per the adopted NFPA 13, and a reference to the owner's inspection program for information. That reference does not design the system.
