# Section 21 05 00 — Common work results for fire suppression (draft)

Draft language for requirements that apply across the Division 21 sections in this pack. Verify with the engineer of record before issue. This section does not elect a system family and it does not size a zone.

## Part 1 — General

### 1.1 Systems this pack will author

Author only these water-sprinkler families, and only after the suppression election names one:

- Wet pipe, Section 21 13 13.
- Dry pipe, Section 21 13 16.
- Double-interlock preaction, Section 21 13 19.

Do not write double-interlock requirements into Section 21 13 16. Pumps are Section 21 30 00. Private mains are Section 21 11 00. Class I standpipes are Section 21 12 00. Fire alarm and releasing are Section 28 31 00. Nitrogen and compressors stay inside the dry-pipe and double-interlock sections.

Foam, clean agent, water mist, and deluge are out of scope. Record the request and stop. Do not size them in these sections. Single-interlock and non-interlock preaction are comparison-only. If either is offered as the basis of design, stop and return an RFI. Do not substitute double-interlock.

A jurisdiction outside the stated NFPA, IBC, and IFC basis is out of scope. Do not port these pins into that row.

### 1.2 Quality assurance and editions

Working plans and hydraulic calculations are prepared by a qualified layout technician under the adopted sprinkler standard, and the installer is a licensed fire-sprinkler contractor. The authority having jurisdiction approves the working plans before fabrication.

Cite the section and the edition on every numeric criterion. An unamended 2024 I-Code floor is the reference pin in this pack (NFPA 13-2022, NFPA 20-2022, NFPA 72-2022, among others). A later edition is a delta unless the adoption says it is enforced. Decide each parameter by the most stringent of the code floor, the insurer sheet, and the owner standard. A judgment the tools did not return is labeled for the engineer of record. It is not written as a code citation.

Do not leave a bracketed project-name or owner blank in an issued paragraph. Do not name a person, a client, or a job. Do not paste a vendor catalog or a model number.

### 1.3 Seismic bracing

Where the seismic design category requires bracing, design is delegated to the sprinkler contractor's engineer for the category shown on the structural drawings. Ask for that category until the structural basis is known. Do not invent it.

Ceilings, not the spacing to schedule: lateral braces at most 40 ft on center (NFPA 13-2025 §18.5.5.2.2); longitudinal braces on feed and cross mains at most 80 ft (§18.5.6.1). Load tables can force a shorter spacing. Gas and fuel piping are not this article. Attachment is the structural engineer's design. NFPA 13-2025 is the delta print. Confirm the enforced edition.

## Part 2 — Products

### 2.1 Shared materials

Pipe, fittings, hangers, braces, and valves are specified in the section that installs them. This section does not add a second product list and does not name a manufacturer.

## Part 3 — Execution

### 3.1 Coordination and identification

Coordination drawings show structure, equipment, and the other trades that share the space. Penetrations are detailed with the rated assembly they pass. Pipe is identified by system and direction of flow. Do not hide a valve, a drain, or a test connection.

Electrically locked egress coordinates with Section 28 31 00. IFC 2024 §1010.2.11: sensor release unlocks on sprinkler or fire detection, stays unlocked until fire-alarm reset, and unlocks on loss of power. IFC 2024 §1010.2.8 is panic and fire-exit hardware (2021 numbering §1010.2.9). Magnetic hold-opens stay on their own path. Do not name a lock.

Spare sprinklers, record drawings, and the contractor's material and test certificate are closeout for the section that installed the system. Owner inspection obligations may be referenced for information. They are not a design calculation.
