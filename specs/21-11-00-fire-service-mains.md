# Section 21 11 00 — Facility fire-suppression water-service piping (draft)

Draft language. Verify with the engineer of record before issue. This section is the private fire service main that feeds risers and pumps. The utility point of connection is coordinated with civil work. Do not duplicate the civil specification.

## Part 1 — General

### 1.1 Summary

Provide private fire service mains, backflow prevention, hydrants, and fire-department connections for a wet, dry, or double-interlock system that has already been elected. A missing sprinkler demand or a missing supply verdict stops the main size. Label a substitute ASSUMED and say why.

NFPA 24 is the code floor. The pack's delta baseline is NFPA 24-2025. An unamended 2024 I-Code adoption typically enforces NFPA 24-2022. Licensed section pins for the 2025 edition are not held, so a 2025 section number is unverified until that book is checked. Do not invent one.

### 1.2 Design criteria

Size the loop for the full sprinkler demand with one leg out of service. Pick diameter from the actual inside diameter, not from the nominal size. Place sectional valves so one impairment does not take the site out of service.

Campus-loop design uses Hazen-Williams C = 100 for every underground material. A material-and-age C table is comparison-only on this section. Do not size the leg on a higher tabulated C. That table may relocate a supply test when the pipe age is known. It does not set the loop's design C.

Needed fire flow is a method election. State which method was adopted. Do not add an unsprinklered flow on top of the sprinkler demand unless the adopted method says to. Do not publish a guessed flow as the main size.

Water-supply test age is not frozen in this pack. Ask for a current test (static pressure, residual pressure, flow, date, and location). Do not hold those blanks in the issued section.

## Part 2 — Products

### 2.1 Pipe and appurtenances

Provide listed pipe, fittings, restraint, hydrants, control valves, and a backflow preventer of the class the water purveyor requires. Do not elect a catalog number. Identify the purveyor's metering, tester registration, and flushing sign-off as process items. They are not a second design basis.

## Part 3 — Execution

### 3.1 Flush and test

Flush underground piping before it is connected to aboveground pipe, and record the flush. Hydrostatic test the main under the adopted NFPA 24. Do not use the fire pump as the test pump. Cover and restraint are installed before the test is accepted. Do not invent a burial depth. If frost cover is missing, label it ASSUMED.
